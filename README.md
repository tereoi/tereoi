## Troy Coenradie
**Fullstack Engineer · Station21**

Self-taught engineer, working across the full stack on Station21 and BizzBoard: system architecture, backend infrastructure, frontend engineering, process automation and AI integration.

### What we build
**BizzBoard** for entrepreneurs, **Station21** for accounting firms. A unified multi-tenant platform that spans the complete operational workflow between businesses and their accountants, encompassing bookkeeping, client management and communication across web and mobile.

### Our goal
Autonomous bookkeeping. Eliminating manual processing entirely, so that every business operates on a continuously current financial position. On that foundation, the full spectrum of financial operations becomes automated, including tax filings, payments, advisory, forecasting and financial modelling.

### AI
Embedded in the core, constrained by deterministic validation and human-in-the-loop review, with tenant-isolated data that is never used to train external models.
